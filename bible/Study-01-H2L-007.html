This is a method to meet together and study a passage from the holy scriptures. First, gather a group of people together to study the scriptures. There are three sections to every meeting, and each one is important. Each section should take the same amount of time.
Section One: Looking back
In the first section the leader begins by asking the group, “What are you thankful for this week?” Allow time for each person to answer.
What are your needs? What are the needs of friends and neighbors?
What can we do to help each other?
Pray for one another
Worship God together
Review the last lesson by asking three questions:
First, ask “What did we learn last week?”
Then ask, “How did you obey what you learned?”
Finally ask, “Who did you share the story with?”
