Section Two: Looking Up - New Scripture
After hearing the new scripture two or three times ask someone in the group to retell the scripture in their own words. After this, ask these four questions:
1 What do you learn from this story?
2 What is difficult to understand in this story?
3 What do you need to obey?
4 Who will you share the story with?
