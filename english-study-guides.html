How to Use the Study Guides

These study guides were designed to help you study and understand God’s Holy Scripture. With the help of God’s Holy Spirit, everyone can understand and obey God’s Word. We can also help each other understand God’s Word. It is best to study God’s Word in a group. Over the past 2,000 years, many people have written teachings to help people understand the Scripture. These study guides combine the God-given wisdom of those many people to help you understand God’s Word. However, the best help for understanding a difficult passage of Scripture is other Scripture. If you find one difficult passage, look for other passages that discuss the same topic or use the same words to help you. Also, ask for God’s help to understand difficult passages. He will help you!
